The composition, palettes, ornaments, shading, and interface in this repository are composed for رَونَق. Diacritics, punctuation, and Latin characters are not rendered as vector marks in this version; the current shaper normalizes them away. This is procedural calligraphic composition, not a fully hand-drawn traditional calligraphy engine.
